Our Story

Since the Beginning

Shree Daan was founded on a single idea — that hospitality is a gift given, not a service sold.

What began as a modest heritage guesthouse in Nashik has grown into a five-star luxury address, without losing the traditional warmth that named it. Every room, every meal, and every welcome still carries that original intention: daan, the act of giving freely.

Foundation (2011)

Shree Daan opens its doors with twelve rooms and one simple promise: guests leave as family.

Expansion (2016)

Introduction of premier suites, in-house fine dining, and our first wellness facilities.

Golden Sparsh (2021)

Launch of the fully integrated Golden Sparsh Spa, blending modern therapies with Vedic massage.

Five-Star Address (Present)

A full 5-star sanctuary of quiet luxury, customized butler service, and award-winning dining.
