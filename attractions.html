Nearby Attractions

Within 80 km

Explore Local Wonders

From rolling vineyards and sacred temples to ancient caves and scenic hilltops, Nashik offers a rich tapestry of day trips that are just a short drive from Shree Daan. Ask our concierge to arrange guided tours, tastings, or a private sunset drive.

🍇

Sula Vineyards

14 km away | India’s most famous winery with tasting rooms, vineyard walks, and fine dining set among gentle slopes.

🛕

Trimbakeshwar Temple

35 km away | One of the twelve Jyotirlingas, framed by cool mountain air and traditional pilgrimage rituals.

🗻

Anjaneri Hill

20 km away | A scenic hilltop spot tied to the birthplace of Lord Hanuman with a quiet temple and sunrise viewpoints.

🪨

Pandavleni Caves

12 km away | A cluster of ancient Buddhist caves carved into the rock, offering history and panoramic valley views.

💧

Gangapur Dam

25 km away | A peaceful lakeside escape ideal for sunset strolls, boat rides, and quiet picnics.

🙏

Shirdi Sai Baba Temple

80 km away | A revered pilgrimage destination with a serene temple complex and spiritual atmosphere.
